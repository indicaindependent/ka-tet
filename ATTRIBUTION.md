# ATTRIBUTION AND ORIGINALITY

This repository is an **independent, unaffiliated homage**. It is not endorsed by,
associated with, or licensed from Stephen King, his publishers, or any rights holder.

## What is borrowed, and what is not

**Borrowed: an idea about structure.** The architecture documented here was designed after
reading *The Dark Tower* novels, and it borrows their *shape* — a central axis held by
radial supports with guarded endpoints, a fellowship bound by a shared task, and a leader
who owes protection to the group and hard decisions to the quest.

**Also borrowed: a handful of terms**, used descriptively — *ka-tet*, *dinh*, *gunslinger*,
*ka-shume*. Individual words and short phrases are not protected by copyright, and they are
used here to *point at* the source, not to pass as it.

**Not borrowed: the text.** No passage from the novels is reproduced in this repository.
Notably, the creed in these pages is an **original composition**. It follows the rhetorical
shape of the gunslinger's catechism — reject the obvious instrument, name the true faculty —
because that shape is the useful part. The words are ours, and the three tenets are paired
with mechanisms that actually run in this system and with the recorded failures that earned
them.

**Not borrowed: the characters.** The avatars are original designs. No character from the
novels is depicted, named, or described. The agents are deliberately unnamed and appear only
as functional seats: the Master, the Dinh, the two Gunslingers, the Archivist.

## Why the agents are anonymous

The operator's agents have working names. They are deliberately **not published here.** What
is useful to a reader is the *architecture* — how a set of AI agents divides authority,
refuses instructions, and verifies each other. Names would add nothing except an attack
surface.
